Job seeker quick help

How do I apply? Open a listing and tap “Apply / View job” to visit the original employer or job-board page. Follow its instructions.

Are these jobs verified? Listings are collected from external sources. Check the employer’s official website, eligibility, deadline and URL before sharing personal information.

Is it free? India Job Alert does not charge candidates to browse listings or apply.

Recruiters: Send a direct message to @india.tech.jobalerts to enquire about sponsored listings. Every listing is subject to verification and approval; paid posts must be labelled sponsored.
